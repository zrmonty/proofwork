# Composition and visual decisions

Use when choosing a direction or correcting an interchangeable layout. These examples guide decisions, not mandatory templates.

| Product | Priority | Revealing failure |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Essay publication | Lead, recent writing, author, readable detail | Equal excerpts obscure lead/byline/action |
| Catalog | Search/filter, comparison, salient attributes | Decorative hero delays comparison |
| Operations console | Status, queue, exceptions, recovery | Marketing headings hide task density |
| Service | Concrete offer, real work, process/contact | Invented credibility replaces evidence |
| Personal tool | Input, result, feedback | Dashboard chrome surrounds one control |

Compare information designs: dominant essay/quiet archive versus chronology; master/detail queue versus focused review. Explain discovery, scanning or reading consequences. Font/color variants alone are not different structures.

## Typography and grouping

Choose roles before fonts: identity, headline, body, metadata, controls and dense data. Families can serve roles through weight, scale and spacing. Test longest supplied headlines/full paragraphs with links, not ideal specimens. Secondary text must remain readable; tiny/faint metadata is not hierarchy.

Reuse suitable incumbent fonts. New families need permitted use, availability, loading and fallback checks. Test preferred/fallback wraps; developer-machine fonts are insufficient. Avoid automatic serif/sans pairings, all-caps or letterspacing repairs. Group headlines, bylines, summaries and actions by meaning.

## Rhythm and shape

Establish within-item, between-related-item and section spacing relationships; vary deliberately for content pauses. Inspect baselines/edges. Random gaps look unfinished; uniform tightness erases grouping.

Use widths appropriate for reading/comparison. Check crops/focal points at narrow sizes. Do not invent media merely to fill space. Use coherent control/media/surface radius roles. Open reading regions can coexist with rounded imagery; nested panels may add noise. Whitespace/dividers/backgrounds can replace cards. Preserve contrast and focus/disabled distinctions.

## Translate references

Extract masthead/lead relationships, metadata placement, archive density, imagery and pauses. Adapt to supplied identity/content; references do not permit copying brands/assets.

Compare directions with identical representative content/difficult titles. Write a thesis linked to audience/task: open reading canvas, author masthead, dominant essay, quiet dated list, restrained rounded subscription control. Carry it through detail/archive/genuine states. Consoles need their own scanning/exception thesis, not imported publication composition.

## Diagnose and refine

Ask whether changing product/content leaves every emphasis unchanged. Familiar patterns can be correct; identify consequences: indistinguishable lead, repeated headings consuming attention, fictional credibility or work displaced by decoration.

Remove regions without jobs. Distinction can come through content relationships, typography, imagery or navigation rather than novelty. Preserve affordances reducing effort.

One masthead, crop strategy, reading rhythm, restrained accent or meaningful ordering can carry identity across index/detail/state views. Avoid unrelated motifs. Refinement preserves recognition and corrects demonstrated hierarchy, rhythm or state defects; wholesale palettes/components may exceed scope.
