# Evidence boundaries

Use when: Selecting verification or interpreting a result.

Choose a check that reaches the claimed user or operational behavior on the current artifact. State what the observation proves and where it stops. Keep failed, unavailable and uncertain observations visible; component success cannot establish an unexercised journey.

Connect claims to commands, interactions or artifacts and name unresolved criteria. Evidence-surface owns reporting; TDD and performance skills own their measurement methods. A short routine report needs no extra ceremony.
