# Design economy

Use when: Choosing, adding or consolidating a mechanism.

Ask one question: what current requirement cannot the existing or simpler mechanism meet? Compare reuse, removal or consolidation, and addition against that requirement and operational ownership. Keep complexity that protects a live contract. Hypothetical growth or reuse cannot justify a component.

Record the decisive requirement, chosen mechanism and condition that would reopen the decision. For cleanup, verify the consumers of removed responsibilities. Architecture-fit owns infrastructure comparisons; language-fit owns runtime comparisons; dead-code and DRY audits own safe deletion and consolidation.
