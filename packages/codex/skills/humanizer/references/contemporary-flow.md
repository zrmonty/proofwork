# Contemporary flow for application copy

Prefer these examples for present-day application explanations and prompts. Read one relevant example,
identify its sentence relationship and write original wording from the product's facts. Use the
[classic novel passages](novel-flow.md) only as optional rhythm comparisons, never as the default register.
A label still needs no prose exercise. Modern does not mean forced slang, contractions or constant warmth.

## Name the object, then its use

GitHub Docs and contributors, *About repositories*. [Source](https://github.com/github/docs/blob/53aac26be558fac4bce599ae94c3ed0542855638/content/repositories/creating-and-managing-repositories/about-repositories.md); [license notice](https://github.com/github/docs/blob/53aac26be558fac4bce599ae94c3ed0542855638/LICENSE) (CC BY 4.0).

> A repository contains all of your code, your files, and each file's revision history. You can discuss and manage your work within the repository.

The definition establishes a repository; the next sentence keeps that same object while explaining what the reader can do. Plain verbs and deliberate repetition preserve continuity. Apply this to an unfamiliar product concept before describing its useful action. Keep your own domain terms; do not inherit GitHub features or the pronouns mechanically.


## Explain the mechanism before the benefit

The Carpentries, *Introducing the Shell*. [Source](https://github.com/swcarpentry/shell-novice/blob/b91d85b848405a4a2fe0a297d8cedb71d68ab9bd/episodes/01-intro.md); [license notice](https://github.com/swcarpentry/shell-novice/blob/b91d85b848405a4a2fe0a297d8cedb71d68ab9bd/LICENSE.md) (CC BY 4.0).

> A command-line interface (CLI) allows users to interact with a computer by reading and writing text. It excels at making repetitive tasks automatic and fast.

The first sentence explains the interaction in everyday terms. It then points back to that mechanism instead of starting a new marketing claim. Use this order in help text: actual capability, then its supported consequence. The quoted lesson is not evidence that your application is fast; do not borrow performance promises.


## Make a question serve the next decision

Nicky Case, *The Evolution of Trust (2017)*. [Source](https://github.com/ncase/trust/blob/6ec45d73befdb922bd40654dd1c1c903a953562f/words.html); [license notice](https://github.com/ncase/trust/blob/6ec45d73befdb922bd40654dd1c1c903a953562f/README.md#license) (CC0 1.0).

> Let's say the other player cheats, and doesn't put in a coin. What should you do?

A contraction and a concrete situation establish an ordinary spoken register. The question matters because an interactive choice follows it. Use conversational prompts when the person actually needs to decide. Do not add questions, suspense or a playful narrator to every screen, and do not copy the game character into a serious error.


## State the situation, then the available choice

Government Digital Service, Crown copyright, *Cookies on GOV.UK*. [Source](https://www.gov.uk/help/cookies); [license notice](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/doc/open-government-licence/version/3/) (Open Government Licence v3.0).

> We use 4 types of cookie. You can choose which cookies you're happy for us to use.

Two direct sentences move from the site's situation to the person's control. Use that relationship when introducing a real preference. Do not copy the number of cookie types or imply every cookie is optional: the surrounding source distinguishes necessary cookies. Preserve your own product's exceptions and actual controls.

## Review the new wording

Keep contemporary vocabulary already appropriate to the product. Check that the next sentence develops
the previous one, referents stay clear and a question has a real purpose. Preserve meaning, permissions and
recovery; do not add a first-person memory, ornate setting or imitation of an author. A source's natural
flow does not prove its facts apply to your product or establish reader satisfaction.
