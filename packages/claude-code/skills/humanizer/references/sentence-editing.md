# Edit the sentence without losing the point

Use for prose that sounds generic, ceremonious, synthetic or hard to scan. Diagnose the mechanism first.
The same word can be useful in one sentence and empty in another; do not run a blacklist over the product.

## Recover the useful information

Ask what the person can learn or do from the sentence. If its nouns and adjectives could describe any app,
replace them with a supplied capability, condition or consequence. If no useful fact remains, remove the
sentence instead of inventing specificity. Keep numbers, limits, ownership and uncertainty exact.

"A powerful way to stay organized" gives no basis for a decision. "Group invoices by client" does, if that
feature exists. An introductory benefit sentence can still earn space when the reader needs its explanation.
Do not turn every paragraph into a button label.

## Expose the action

Prefer a person or system doing something over abstract nouns doing the explanatory work. Replace
"Completion of verification is required prior to continuation" with "Verify your email to continue" when
that is the real gate. Keep a passive construction when the actor is unknown, irrelevant or unsafe to name.

Move caveats next to the promise they qualify. Avoid a confident headline followed by a footnote that
reverses it. Do not hide a fee, retention limit or destructive consequence merely to improve sentence rhythm.

## Remove performed persuasion

Inspect stock invitations, inflated intensifiers, repeated rhetorical questions, dramatic contrasts and
three-part benefit strings. Their problem is information displaced by a familiar cadence. Replace a
formula only when it obscures the actual point. A real three-option choice should remain a clear list.

Remove "we know how you feel" when the product has no basis for that claim. Describe the situation and
useful help instead. Avoid unsupported team activity such as "we're already fixing it" or false certainty
such as "nothing was lost." Do not add jokes, slang, anecdotes or mistakes to imitate human authorship.

## Read the whole passage

For concrete demonstrations, prefer [contemporary examples](contemporary-flow.md);
[novel passages](novel-flow.md) offer optional rhythm comparisons. Read one relevant example, observe how
information connects, then write original copy from product facts. Keep the product register; narrative
decoration and fictional experience are not interface requirements.

Check whether neighboring lines all start the same way, restate the same conclusion or end in identical
calls to action. Combine fragments that belong together; split sentences with competing jobs. Keep deliberate
parallelism for choices, steps and stable controls. Vary rhythm only where meaning benefits.

Use contractions when the audience and product register support them. Do not force them into formal notices.
Keep precise domain terms and recognizable action names; arbitrary synonyms can make one feature look like
several. An em dash, semicolon or ordinary transition is not evidence of machine writing.

## Compare meaning, then stop

Check the rewrite against the supplied facts and observed behavior. Ask whether anything became more certain,
less consequential, more personal or less precise. Read aloud for awkwardness, then review the surrounding
screen. Accept clear wording when remaining alternatives are taste. Shorter is not inherently better if it
removes the reason, object or safe next step.
