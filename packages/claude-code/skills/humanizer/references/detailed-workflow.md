# Humanizer editorial workflow

Make visible application wording specific, accurate and useful for the person at that moment. A label can do more than a welcome paragraph. Copy work does not authorize behavior changes or removal of required facts.

## Understand the context

Read the real screen, surrounding flow, terminology and content constraints. Identify audience knowledge, intended action and whether the moment is routine, uncertain or consequential. Follow the established suitable voice; ask only about unresolved meaning that changes copy. For inconsistent products, record a short audience/vocabulary note and representative action/help/error lines rather than a personality workshop.

Preserve prices, permissions, limits, legal meaning, disclosure and actual capabilities. Distinguish fixtures from published claims. Never invent quotes, experience, popularity, urgency, familiarity or guarantees to sound authentic. Natural language does not certify human authorship.

## Give content a job

Use [structure and voice](structure-and-voice.md) for composition. Each region should orient, inform, enable action, navigate or help recovery. Remove repeated explanations and decorative labels while retaining decision facts around cost, access, loss and next steps.

Headings should separate meaningful topics, not supply an eyebrow/title/subtitle/paragraph for every control. Preserve semantic heading order and accessible landmarks. Headers/footers should contain actual navigation, accounts, required links or publication identity; avoid duplicate navigation, marketing footers inside focused tasks and recurring slogans. A masthead/archive may serve a publication, while checkout needs focus. Choose by the page's purpose.

Review repetition across the journey. Keep deliberate terminology consistent; vary sentence rhythm without renaming the same action on each screen.

## Plain, specific language

Use [sentence editing](sentence-editing.md) to diagnose abstraction, unsupported persuasion and repetitive
cadence. Use [voice calibration](voice-calibration.md) for unsettled register or high-stakes tone; read
[worked revisions](worked-revisions.md) when comparing a candidate against its supplied facts. Keep these
conditional rather than loading every example for a single-label correction.

Use concrete nouns and active verbs: “Export invoices” communicates the action. Replace unsupported praise and stock introductions with actual information, or delete them. Contractions and specialist terms should fit audience and established register. Read aloud for stiffness without adding filler, mistakes, forced humor, slang or invented anecdotes.

Routine success can be “Changes saved.” Payment failure needs truthful status/recovery. Remove unearned promises and avoid repetitive three-benefit lists, dramatic contrasts or closing slogans. Connected prose suits explanations, lists suit parallel choices, tables suit comparisons; formatting follows the reader's task.

## Interaction and state contracts

Read [interaction copy](interaction-copy-and-review.md) for actions, accessibility and localization, and [messages as contracts](message-contracts.md) for partial/dynamic/cross-surface results. Labels describe actual effects: save draft, send invitation, delete the named object. Empty, filtered and failed-load states differ. Queued work is not completed work; uncertain payment must not invite another charge.

Labels stand alone; placeholders supply examples. Put consequential guidance before avoidable errors. Preserve localization IDs, interpolation, plural/escaping/formatting contracts and required consent/privacy/security language. Propose material legal changes for designated review instead of silently changing meaning.

## Finish in context

Compare controls, confirmations, toasts and notifications for conflicting promises. Preserve disclosure policies and safe public errors. Review ordinary/failure states together for repetition, terminology, heading density and understandable next actions.

For implemented wording, inspect representative narrow/wide layouts, long labels, affected accessible names and error placement where tools permit. Exercise behavior that copy depends on; reuse localization/behavior checks rather than freezing incidental phrasing. Report unavailable rendering, interaction or language review honestly. Source inspection is not rendered verification.

Return revised copy or working change, material structural decisions, checks and unresolved facts. Judge usefulness and truth, not an AI-detector score or authorship guarantee.

For the final comparison, identify any changed certainty, object, scope, time, permission or next step.
If the sentence became friendlier by weakening a consequence or promising unsupported recovery, reject it.
Stop when remaining alternatives express preference rather than correcting an actual defect.
