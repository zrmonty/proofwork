# Frontend direction and craft revision — 0.10.1

Deepens the originally authored frontend-design skill and its composition/rendered-review references, adds a conditional interface-craft reference, and aligns its specification, scenario rubrics and README description. Both packages remain twenty-one skills. No design dependency, template library or runtime change was added.

## Decisions strengthened

- An actionable visual thesis expresses content priority, discovery, character and durable visual choices. References inform mechanisms rather than copying another identity.
- A substantial build establishes a representative vertical slice with real content before multiplying sections; index, detail, controls and relevant states share the direction.
- Typography is inspected as role relationships, with actual paragraphs, long headings, dense values, font availability and fallback behavior.
- Composition uses deliberate axes, grouping, spacing and density. Repeated regions need a job; a habitual promotional sequence cannot substitute for supplied content.
- Surface softness is selective and coherent. Open reading regions, rounded media/controls and related nested corners must preserve focus, contrast and usable state distinctions.
- Images/icons need purpose, permitted use, meaningful crops and missing-asset behavior. Generated decoration cannot masquerade as documentary or customer evidence.
- Components include actual interaction states, focus, error/recovery and data-dependent transitions. Pure decisions and canonical state preserve backend truth; polish cannot invent undo, progress or success.
- Iteration records a visible defect, a predicted remedy and an observed comparison under matched content/viewports. Intermediate widths, realistic content and regressions remain relevant; no palette cycling, novelty quota or subjective certification.

Additional acceptance scenarios cover a templated publication hiding its lead and a polished form with clipped errors, hidden focus and false success state. These are authored rubrics, not executed browser evidence.

## Limits

This release updates design guidance, not a target interface. No browser composition, visual comparison, keyboard flow, asset fallback, accessibility measurement or user evaluation was executed. Package checks establish structural availability; they do not prove visual improvement, authentic character, originality or reader satisfaction. The skill keeps these claims conditional on observed target-artifact evidence.
