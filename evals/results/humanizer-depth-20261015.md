## Changed editorial contract

- Diagnose missing information, abstraction and performed persuasion before replacing words.
- Preserve the product's register and domain terms while adapting tone to the person and moment.
- Read adjacent sentences for cadence, redundant conclusions and unsupported intimacy; keep genuine
  parallel choices and useful punctuation rather than enforcing a blacklist.
- Ground worked revisions in supplied facts. Review changes to certainty, object, scope, time,
  permissions and recovery before accepting a smoother or shorter sentence.
- Preserve useful page structure, accessibility, localization and authoritative state. Keep engineering
  reports/docs with clear-voice rather than expanding humanizer into a catch-all writing skill.

show supported specificity, formal voice, queued work, uncertain outcomes, useful empathy, structure,
retention/permission precision and patterns that should remain unchanged. Their stated assumptions are
hypothetical product facts, not newly established application behavior or a default policy.
