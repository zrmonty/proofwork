# Interface craft

Use actual components/content to resolve a selected direction. These are diagnostic decisions, not a fixed kit.

## Typography at use

Inspect headline, body, metadata, labels, controls and dense values together. Establish readable weight, line height and measure before adding families. Test longest titles without hard breaks for one viewport. Check paragraphs/links/lists/captions with preferred/fallback fonts; secondary text remains legible.

Align form/table labels, units and values by meaning; use numeric alignment for comparison. Avoid truncating distinguishing text or shrinking to fit: choose wrapping, disclosure or another appropriate arrangement. Check zoom, localization/long scripts where scoped. Verify loading/glyphs/weights rather than synthesis or developer fonts.

## Relationships and density

Choose an organizing edge/axis; optical exceptions should improve visible alignment rather than accumulate arbitrary offsets. Group titles/descriptions, labels/fields, errors/actions and media/captions through spacing before boxes. Nested panels need meaningful boundaries.

Density serves tasks: reading can breathe, queues must expose actionable items. Empty space clarifies emphasis rather than hiding work. Narrow layouts preserve priorities/relationships. Asymmetry can distinguish leads or workspace rails; equivalent comparisons may need symmetry. Neither proves originality.

## Surfaces and softness

Assign text, muted text, surface, divider, accent, focus and feedback roles. Accent everywhere defeats emphasis. Status needs wording/shape beyond color. Borders/shadows/backgrounds express grouping/depth; avoid combined effects without jobs. Preserve contrast, identity and fallback.

Relate radii to scale/padding. Selective rounded controls/media can invite use while reading remains open; nested pills create noise. Review hover/focus/selected/pending/invalid/disabled families beyond tiny opacity changes. Clipping, sticky elements and decoration must not hide focus or intercept input.

## Truthful assets

Inventory permitted imagery/logos/icons. Choose meaning, coherent crop/focal point/aspect/captions; test missing/slow assets and narrow crops. Generated decoration cannot impersonate customers, documentary evidence, testimony or product screenshots. Record provenance/placeholders; text-only can beat irrelevant stock media.

Keep icon weight/meaning coherent and labels clear for unfamiliar/consequential actions. Derive charts/diagrams from supplied facts, not invented numbers.

## Complete interaction families

Fields need label/value/help/error/recovery; selectable rows need identity/selection/consequences; dialogs need initial/return focus, keyboard, complete/cancel using established accessible mechanisms. Essential instructions cannot depend on tooltips.

Place feedback near action. Motion explains continuity but cannot imply unconfirmed success or hide delay. Preserve reduced motion/static states. Check focus/responsiveness when content inserts/disappears/reorders. Hover cannot gate touch tasks or shift neighbors. Sticky chrome must not cover focus/recovery; reserve loading space without fictional progress.

## Integrate

Reuse tokens/primitives and add needed roles only. Remove obsolete overrides/styles/assets after tracing dynamic consumers. Where relevant, measure image/font loading, effects and shifts on representative devices/tools; screenshots do not prove runtime cost. Preserve semantics/accessibility rather than generic styled containers replacing controls to match a comp.
