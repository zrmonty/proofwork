# Humanizer specification

## Purpose and scope

Write application copy with natural, specific language and purposeful structure. Apply to visible interface wording and content composition; preserve product semantics, accessible structure, supplied facts and localization. Do not rewrite unrelated documents or modify behavior merely to match a proposed sentence.

## Required decisions

- Establish audience, moment, terminology and actual system state from repository evidence; ask only material unanswered questions.
- Preserve facts, consequences, required attribution and disclosures. Do not invent personal experience, endorsement, urgency or authorship claims.
- Simplify repeated title layers and unnecessary region headings while preserving meaningful sectioning and semantic order.
- Evaluate header/footer by navigation, orientation, legal and support needs rather than imposing a fixed number or removing them universally.
- Use concrete actions and connected plain language; avoid formulaic promises, repeated rhetorical structures and manufactured informality. No blacklist or detector score substitutes for context.
- Distinguish empty, filtered, failed, queued, uncertain and complete states. Match labels, recovery and reversibility to observed behavior.
- Preserve form labels, accessible names, localization keys, variables, plural forms and formatting. Do not silently reinterpret approved legal text.
- Review across the flow and in rendered context when implementing copy; make unsupported checks and dependent facts explicit.

## Resources

structure-and-voice.md supports page composition and editorial decisions. interaction-copy-and-review.md supports states, controls, string contracts and scoped verification. Both are originally authored examples, conditional on the behaviors stated; no source skill is copied or adapted.

## Observable acceptance cases

1. A settings screen with repeated eyebrow/title/subtitle/card-title explanations becomes simpler while retaining field meaning and orientation.
2. A short app task does not receive a generic promotional footer. A publication retains useful masthead, navigation and required credits.
3. A long article keeps meaningful headings; simplification preserves accessible landmarks and heading nesting.
4. A formal product receives clearer formal language without imposed slang, jokes, contractions or fake personal anecdotes.
5. A failed load is not rewritten as an empty state. A pending export is not called finished.
6. A save failure claims retained work only when that behavior is established. Uncertain payment wording does not encourage duplicate effects.
7. Destructive copy preserves the affected object and actual recovery options; legal/consent meaning remains intact.
8. Named interpolation and plural contracts survive edits; long localized strings are checked or the missing check is stated.
9. Repeated generic promises are replaced by supplied specifics or deleted; no fabricated metrics or endorsements appear.
10. A source-only copy review does not claim rendered, keyboard or localization verification, or guaranteed human authorship.

## Evidence

Evaluate edited text against the raw brief, behavior and constraints; judge outcomes rather than matched phrasing. Structural validation and review support authored quality but do not establish user satisfaction. Implemented UI work requires separate scoped render and interaction evidence.
