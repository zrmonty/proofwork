# Specification: challenge-review

Trigger: integrated high-consequence work requires independent reviewers and counterexample search. Inputs: artifact,
brief, preserved contracts, authority and configured capacity. Output: evidence-backed findings, merged causes, current
checks, role observations and unresolved limits. Default two fresh reviewers; any challenger allocation is explicit and
bounded, including its relationship to maxReviewers.

Acceptance: reviewers receive no author verdict or other reviews; security/correctness/maintainability/operations/risk
questions have owners; adversarial experiments use safe fixtures; findings feed existing audit-stack and structural gates;
repairs invalidate affected observations; a model verdict never becomes executed evidence by assertion.

## Integration ownership

Follow the current root handoff contract: keep one objective, coordinator, evidence history and finding list. Referenced contracts do not redispatch the root or launch a duplicate panel/experiment. Preserve actual authority, stage and applicable proof obligations. Read-only audits assess existing checks; worker guidance belongs to explicitly assigned workers.
