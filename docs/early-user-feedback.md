# Early-user feedback loop

Invite a small opt-in group operating systems with uptime, recovery, security or change-control obligations. This prepares the process; no recruitment outcomes or participants are claimed.

Start with a public-safe or synthetic task resembling actual work. Agree scope, data handling, observation window and publication consent. Do not request production credentials, private customer records or unrestricted access. Participation can stop without public explanation.

Record the outcome in ordinary language, host/source version, authority, observed friction, independently verified result, residual operator work, defects/rework and whether the participant would ship in the stated environment. Distinguish necessary product choices from avoidable technical questionnaires. Feedback is not compliance certification.

Use the [paired protocol](user-outcome-evaluation.md) for comparisons. Review traces and consent before attribution; default to anonymized aggregates. Templates and synthetic fixtures are not testimonials.

Reproduce one friction point safely, make the smallest adequate repair and rerun the affected outcome. Invite assessment of the concrete result. Release bounded improvements with honest evidence, preserving adverse findings and unusable rows. Do not expand commands merely because someone used a new term.

Success stories link public-safe before/after work, executed checks, remaining responsibilities and the observation window. Distinguish local, staged and verified-live work. Never invent ship approval, defect rates or reduced operational risk.
